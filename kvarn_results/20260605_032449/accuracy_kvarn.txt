Q5: Briefly name Newton's three laws of motion.
A:  Newton's three laws of motion are:

1. **First Law (Law of Inertia):** An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion with the same speed and direction unless acted upon by an unbalanced force.

2. **Second Law (Law of Acceleration):** The acceleration of an object is directly proportional to the net force acting on it and inversely proportional to its mass. Mathematically, $ F = ma $, where $ F $ is force, $ m $ is mass, and $ a $ is acceleration.

3. **Third Law (Action-Reaction Law):** For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. Forces always occur in pairs.
